# Roblox Development

I helped lead a small coding team building reusable Roblox game templates and contributed to a separate catalog of asset packs.

## Contribution

I worked with a three-person coding team inside a five-person studio. I helped plan and debug Luau systems for player-data persistence, NPC placement and collection, reusable interfaces, and mobile controls.

## Results

- **Team templates:** Games using our templates reached **258.6K peak concurrent players** and **1.5B+ combined visits**.
- **Asset packs:** Our catalog had **100+ paid team sales**. Games using the packs reported **330K+ combined concurrent players**.

These figures describe two different groups of games and are team or downstream results, not a claim that I built or operated every finished game.
